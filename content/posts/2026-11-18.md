I haven't done much. Just work, but as I said, wow, I really needed that vacation. Felt so much better. And I'm just going on with life, and life seems to be getting better. Need to lock in for my exams though. I didn't do terrible on my midterms, but definitely much below my expectations. Makes sense though. I've dived into work, but I'm hardly working. Saw Max, saw Dylan, saw people, played soccer, yeah... Started walking places more, slowing down and enjoying the little things again. 

It's been a good two weeks. It's the best I've felt. I was struggling with a big decision, but I was also really just feeling peaceful, and I didn't want to disturb that peace. Eventually though, I made my decision. I touched the flame again. I knew it would hurt, and it did. But I needed to know, needed to tell myself, that I tried. That I tried my best, that I gave everything. So I don't regret my decision. 

It has come today, but it's just grief. Temporary, nothing I haven't seen before. It's pretty funny to think about. Sitting in class, not that I pay much attention either, but listening to lecture on strong markov property while a tear silently rolls down my cheek. 

Let it be. I really tried my best. Given it my all.
