Last 10 days, I've spent half of it studying nonstop for my (actually important) midterms. And then I took those midterms (which didn't go well, but didn't go not well either). Man discovers that Ph.D classes are difficult. 

Then, immediately afterwards, I hopped on a flight to SF, for INFORMS. Well, first, to see my friend Ryan, who I stayed with over the weekend. He showed, and drove me around the bay. It was extremely decompressing, extremely relaxing. And, it was extremely nice to finally talk to somebody real again. These past two months have been the busiest, but also the loneliest few months of my life so far. So it was very nice to take a break from that, see a good friend, and have a nice vacation to myself.

We went to see some of the nature in the bay. And I also realized, I've been in the city too much, and need to see more nature. Was actually very healing, I instantly felt relaxed just, driving along the landscapes, things quieting down. City life is nice, but it is very nice to take a break from that. Need to prioritize getting sunlight, touching grass. 

Then, I went to INFORMS, and presented. Saw old and new faces, it was a lot of fun. Much better experience than I had at IOS this march. Presentation went pretty well too. 

Now, I'm currently on the flight back to NY. I slept in the SFO airport, haha, for my 6 a.m flight. Was honestly very optimal, not bad.

Anyway. I'm feeling quite good at the moment, have been feeling very good this entire extended weekend, doing all these things. Been busy, but also feeling very at ease. I've been pondering this question, where I felt that I was moreso heading towards an affirmative answer the past few weeks. But after this weekend, after feeling so at ease, I don't know. When I feel like this, I feel like, I don't want to disrupt this peace. Because the alternative I know feels much worse. And I know there's no lack of love, and I know that either of my answer's won't change that.

So I don't know. Finally feeling peace. Let them. Let them do whatever they want. I obviously still hold the desire, what I want most in the world. But it's OK. It finally feels like I can hold the two truth's in my hands, without either burning my palms. I hope this marks the begining of me feeling at peace. There are probably still going to be waves, lapses, but, this is the first wave of peace I've felt. And it feels good.
