Ah, what a week. What a few days. I've done basically nothing, except walk around, and shop. And then stay at home, and browse online shopping. It's just shopping. Spent so much money. Don't get me started on what I just bought and how much it was (you don't want to know). 

I've also been finishing up my paper. Been hard at work on that, grinding. I also went back to Boston for two days to help Jaden move in. Was fun. Everytime we hangout, we get into some sort of debacle. This time it was the hour spent moving mattress from the basement. Don't get me started on that, and whose basement it was. 

I also saw Jason the other day. Pretty fun talking to him. But other than that, I've been alone, just shopping. I am now, eagerly awaiting orientation, and school to start. But school doesn't start until another two weeks! Did not realize that, FDOC is after labor day. Just checked today. Now I'm like, what am I going to do for another two weeks? Fortunately orientation is next week, so we'll see. 

Really feel like peter parker. Mostly alone in a big new city. It's not been bad, but yeah, I need to keep busy.

Am I arrogant to say that I have lived my life almost exactly according to the way I have wanted to live it? In terms of everything I can control. I feel like most people don't. I was also told a while ago that I was a unicorn, that I was mostly morally consistent, and most people were not. Seems somewhat related. Some rules for me. Do no (unjust) harm to those I care about. I think I've held to that pretty well. Do good for those you care about. That may be a new one now, and a modified one. It may just be: Do good. What else? Modesty? I don't know. But I think that my 18 year old self, my 14 year old self, my 10 year old self would be happy to see the person I am. I am perhaps arrogant, perhaps not self seeing, to say that I have lived my life to a close maximum to the right way, herein defined by myself. 

Good things are coming. They already have. Karma? Or my (mostly) indominatable belief that everything will work out. I just need to keep living the right way. I am mostly sure that the new semester, this new life, will do me good, thus: I am waiting with bated breath.
