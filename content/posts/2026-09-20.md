Still very busy. The second week quieted down a little, but then I've figured out how to fill time pretty well (fitness classes!), so it's been pretty good. Also meal prepping a lot. I've seeing a counselor now. I don't know how I feel about it yet. 

As I said before, my life is objectively good, busy, packed. Doing so much stuff, doing a good job, doing everything I could correctly. But honestly, I think I've even been getting worse these days. This will very likely become the most difficult period of my life (so far), as well as the most rewarding. Really going through it. 

Let me just recap what I've done since - Went bowling (doesn't really count), played heck lot of soccer (making new friends), played squash, started therapy, going to fitness classes, hanging out in the office, cooking, biking places, seeing friends (Jason in LIC this past week), starting research, starting homework, studying, everything. OK.

The people at wellness services kept making sure I wasn't going to kill myself. I kept telling them that I was not at all suicidal. I just feel pretty terrible, pretty often. Honestly, I'm wondering why I wouldn't kill myself. I think that I am too rational to ever seriously consider it. Again, objectively looking at my life, I know that my life is very fortunate, and that it is with great likelihood, only going to get better. So there's that. And also, it's not like I ever think something along the lines of: "The suffering is just too much, I can't stand it, I'd be better off not existing, therefore not feeling the pain". 1. Because I think I have a pretty high suffering tolerance. 2. I am really scared of death, I think I'd much rather suffer than not exist, unless I was in an unbearable amount of pain, every second, like torture. And that leads back to (1), which is, that my unbearable amount of pain is pretty darn high. There are still many moments, when I'm not busy, where the pain returns in a dull ache. I can deal with that. 

I am still suffering and not doing great. Honestly, feel like it's been even getting worse, even though my life has been getting better. Been quite sensitive lately, much more sensitive than even 1 or 2 months ago. So idk. I don't think I will ever kill myself, nor consider it at all. But at the moment, I feel like I am giving so much to the world, yet the world is not giving me life back. For the unlikely event that you bother to come across any of these entries, or an automated web crawler does, know that, i'm doing my best, haha. I hope that if I ever come back to these words (which I probably will not), I know that I'll probably be in a better place then. But the version of myself now, is suffering. I don't know what I'll think then. I think most likely, future me will not want to revisit these dark times. But for documentations sake, for the person I may become, here it is.
