---
title: Practicing Gratitude
date: 2026-10-18
---

That I am alive.

That I am in good health.

That I may feel the breeze across my face as I bike. The sun on my skin. 

That I am an observer of this world. Of people.

That I have not lost myself.

That I may look towards the future with hope, and know my life will most likely, be better.

That I live within myself.
