Quick recap. Went to a boat cruise. Was pretty fun (the first hour), then wanted to go home. Went painting. Went biking. Playing soccer. Going to lots of gym classes. Making friends (hopefully). Cooking. Making good ice cream. Ahh. 

 I think that therapy makes me feel worse. At least temporarily. Two sessions now. The first session sent me into a deep funk, for four, five days. The worst I've felt in months. I was just scrolling, and something would affect me, and then the algorithm would know that, and then keep pushing things that would affect me. A self fulfilling loop, chicken and egg scenario. And it was really, really affecting me. 

 My second session sent me into that loop too. But only for a day. I realized I was going back into that funk, and quickly made my algorithm better. 

 Mood is still very low. And things are very contradictory. I saw an older women on the subway the other day, silently crying and wiping her tears away. I stood there for a few stops, just wondering, should I try to help in any way? Ask her if she's OK? And I realized again, this is part of what being an adult is about. At least to me. So I went up to her and just checked up on her. Wished her well with whatever she was dealing with (work apparently). And you know, this was something I wouldn't have done in a million years, before. 

 You know, I did this not because I'm trying to be a good person. Well maybe a little. But no, it was mostly for the endorphin hit I was expecting, and for any potential social credit.But I didn't get any endorphin hit at all. In fact, it made me so much sadder. They say perhaps, that empathy is a two way street. Maybe I helped her absorb some sadness. But I have very little capacity for that at the moment, and it really, destabilized me as well. Doing such an act, made me feel really, really sad. 

 Why? I'm not entirely sure. Probably because it was a reminder of just, how much I've changed. How much I've grown, for the better. And just, what it took to get here. I generally like the person I've become. I am the same person as before. But I understand more now. More mature. More sad. I don't know what is killing me. I know, but I don't know. 

 It's already October. Busy with classes. So much freaking homework. Doing a little bit of research. Life is generally very fun, very busy. And I'm exhausted. I'm really exhausted. I think I may burn out soon. As I previously mentioned, the low upper bound. I thought that I'd be improving, that I'd be making that upper bound gradually higher. But I'm really getting tired. The cycle repeats itself. I stay busy, I have fun, I hit the low upper bound, but it builds in the background. I let it build, I cry, I release, and I go backwards a little. And I repeat. And this cycle happens every 4-7 days, and yeah. At this point, it's the same cycle. Before with this cycle, it felt like I was improving the low upper bound. Now it feels like I keep hitting the same wall. And that is gradually, killing me.
