I finally finished my paper, submitted two hours ago haha. Always a grind, the last week, I'm noticing. Proud to have done so. 

I also have been doing orientation the past few days. Last week, the first & second day of orientation were pretty freaking bad lol. It was like a birthday party for all the masters students. I've been dying to meet some fellow Ph.D students, and nope, didn't get the chance to when masters students outnumber us like 50:1 here. A gigantic waste of time, sat through 3 hours of advertisements.

This week should be better. Today, I had my first Ph.D only orientation and actually got to talk to people. I tried my best to be social, and I was quite social, though by the end of it, I was quite drained. 3 more days of this, hopefully I can make a few friends. 

Today, talking to people, hearing about how life will be, how it can be, I really bought into the jungle juice. Walked away feeling pretty upbeat. And I should be upbeat. 

I also played soccer on Saturday. Fun, quite fun. Although I saw somebody get injured quite badly, was a little scary. 

Now that I guess, the busyness of finishing the paper is gone, I'll need to do more orientation stuff, and then school. I'm settling in quite good, I think. Been cooking, making ice cream, etc. 

Honestly, it feels like I've lived another lifetime since, idk, maybe two lifetimes, since summer, since graduation. All in, what, four months? five months? I was scrolling through my old pictures of this summer. Had a nice photo of the 长江 boat tour, and I was like. Wow. I remember that. Taking those photos. Putting shades on, gazing at the beautiful landscapes. Listening to let down by radiohead on repeat. The elderly, the kids, the families all enjoying themselves besides me, and me, sitting there, just staring at the water. Pondering. 

That feels like another lifetime ago. 

Yeah I mean, I don't know where I'm going with this.
