---
title: An exercise in thanks on Sunday
date: 2026-10-11
---

That I am alive, living. That I am who I am. 

That I am able to readyily enjoy the fruits of others: Music, technology, literature, knowledge.

That I possess eyes that can hope to see the soul.

That I am in good health. 

That I experienced love, truly.

That I have grown up in an environment that has allowed me to (almost) maximize my potential.

That I have had the opportunity and privilege to not know what to pursue, and to freely have chosen to pursue what I truly want.

That I have the means to help those in more need than I. And the philosophy to do so. And the recognition that my position is very fortunate.

That I have seen much of the world. That I have experienced much that others haven't. 

That I have been blessed with a fair share of talent. A good amount of drive. A bit of luck. And a lot of opportunity.

That I have/had friends that are good. That I have learned from, that have kept me from growing arrogant.

That my perspective, my personality, prevents me from being willfully, naively ignorant.

That my sister was the type of sister to pave her own road, a road which I would make more turns on.

That I am still young, with much to learn, much to see, much to live.

That I enjoy the sports, soccer, biking, exercise.

That my grandmother took care of me without expectation.

That I earn my own money, of which I am being paid to learn.

That my dog provided me with companionship in some of my loneliest moments.

That I am not terribly ill tempered.

That I have known a good life.
