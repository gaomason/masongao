title: The low upper bound
date: 2026-09-12
---
I've been so freaking busy since school has started. OK, so I mean, we had general Ph.D orientation last week. Met a lot of new people, but all pretty shallowly, it was mostly just networking for a few days. Terrible food. But I put in the effort to be very social, and I was quite social. Always very drained by the end of the day though. Not much to write home about really.. Talked to a lot of people and it was fun, I was having a pretty nice time. 

Then classes started this week. And our department is also doing a lot of events. And I'm exhausted. If I thought last week, I was being social, well this week, I am on social plus plus plus. If you know me, I am probably the most anti-social person you know. And I am probably one of the most social persons in the program at the moment. I'm doing it out of necessity, but also, kind of, to prove that it isn't so hard, it was always a lack of desire rather than capability. And that has been shown to be very true. Although, I am so brain tired now. 

I'm also so excited to get started on research and stuff. I think last semester, I was actually so burnt out. Like crazy. I didn't want to do anything academic. But now, I'm ready to get back into it. I think I have a lot of energy.

I'm doing so much stuff. Being social at school/work. Playing soccer and making friends. Playing squash (hopefully more). I went to work a shift as a line cook at a soup kitchen today too. Like just random stuff. Going to seminars. Going to department events. Socializing (Did I mention I'm socializing?) Going to networking dinners (OK i'm not going to ever do that again, unless the food is good. The one I went to this week, the food was dog). 

Part of me is a little afraid that I'm doing these things just to distract myself. At the end of the day, the hole still remains, when I'm biking home, when the noise dies down. And idk how to approach that. 

I kind of thought, that with my new life, my happiness would just go up. But as I like to say, I think that I am near the maximum of a pretty low upper bound. I thought that the bound would increase, and maybe it will, slowly. But I thought it would go up more, with all these things I'm doing. But in the end, they still seem to be distractions. I'm only 2 weeks in though, so time will tell. I'm trying to start seeing a therapist next week. I think that could help. I thought maybe, that I can get through my difficulties mostly by myself, but this low ceiling that I've seemed to hit has made me question that. 

Like my life is objectively, pretty great right now. Doing a lot of stuff. Excited about a lot of stuff. I have lots of other things to write about. Research. Potential PI's. Classes. Socializing. Homework. People. Lots of stuff, and lots to like. But in terms of how I feel, it's just not all the way up there. 

It's a marathon, not a sprint. That's what they say about a Ph.D. But I think it applies to life in general. With all these things I'm doing, I'm trying to be careful of doing too much and burning out. I already feel pretty tired, I ended up doing too much today. Need to use tomorrow to recover. Also, ai math is crazy. Everyone is freaking out about it. Scary times for me, for us.

Haha! You know what is really funny/crazy? Of my incoming cohort of 18 students, I'm pretty sure I'm the only domestic student. And I'm only technically domestic. Crazy. 

Oh. My paper was rejected last week too. More work! Not relevant enough to the journal, which is fair. More work!
